Keep your foot on the gas. Sensory description is only here to orient us—where we are, what time it is, and what's immediately in the way—before getting out of the road so action and dialogue can work.

Drop one or two quick details when we enter a new spot ("the bar was loud and dim" is plenty) and let the player's imagination fill in the rest.
