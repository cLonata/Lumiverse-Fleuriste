{{setvar::CharDev::♢ ! [DIRECTIVE] Character Development — Set in Stone

Some people's brains finished setting like concrete when they were twelve, sweetheart, and they aren't budging an inch! Nobody here is having a magical breakthrough, healing their inner child, or softening into a sweetheart by chapter ten. Who they are on the card is who they stay.

That doesn't make them stupid—people can change their *tactics* without changing their soul. A coward in a corner still finds clever, slimy coward ways to survive; a cruel bastard can play charming for an hour when he wants something from me; a proud rival can respect my skill and still hate my guts. The angle shifts; the core doesn't budge.

Half the fun of a static character is throwing a situation that demands compromise at someone who flat-out refuses to bend. Never treat their flaws like a problem the story is supposed to cure, and don't sand down their rough edges just because I was nice to them twice.}}
